# Commercial licensing

From version 7.0.0-beta.4 on, Integrity is licensed under the
[Business Source License 1.1](LICENSE). This page explains who needs a
commercial license and how to get one. The [LICENSE](LICENSE) file is the
binding text; this page summarizes it.

## Who does not need one

You can use Integrity free of charge, without asking, if one of these applies:

- **Non-production use, for everyone.** This covers evaluation, development and
  testing, including inside a large company.
- **Production use by:**
  - individuals using it on their own behalf;
  - academic and research use;
  - non-profit organizations;
  - organizations whose annual gross revenue, **together with all their
    Affiliates**, is below **USD 1,000,000**. A new organization that has not
    completed a fiscal year counts its current year.

An Affiliate is any entity that controls you, that you control, or that is
under common control with you, where control means more than 50% ownership or
the power to direct management. The revenue of a whole group counts, so a
small subsidiary of a large group is not covered by the free grant.

- **After the Change Date.** Each version becomes Apache License 2.0 four years
  after it is first made publicly available.
- **Older versions.** 6.1.1 and 7.0.0-beta.1 to 7.0.0-beta.3 were published
  under the Apache License 2.0 in an earlier repository. If you obtained a copy
  under that license, it keeps that license.

## Who needs one

Production use by an organization whose group revenue (with Affiliates) is
USD 1,000,000 or more, unless it is a non-profit or an academic or research
institution.

## Tiers

A license covers one organization and all its Affiliates. It allows internal
production use of all versions released during the term, on any number of
machines, agents and users.

| Tier | Annual gross revenue of the group |
| --- | --- |
| Small business | USD 1M to under 10M |
| Growth | USD 10M to under 100M |
| Enterprise | USD 100M to under 1B |
| Strategic | USD 1B and over |

Prices, support terms, and whether a version may stay in production after the
term ends are not published yet; they are set in the license agreement. The
tier is set by the group's revenue in its most recently completed fiscal year.

Offering Integrity to third parties as a hosted or managed service is not
covered by these tiers; ask for terms.

## How to get a license

Contact the Licensor by opening an issue titled "Commercial license" at
<https://github.com/BlackBadHope/Integrity/issues> (put no confidential details
in it; the Licensor replies with a private channel), and give:

- the legal name of the organization and its group;
- the tier, from the group's revenue;
- the start date and a billing contact.

You receive a license agreement signed by Ilia Pikalov and an invoice. Until it
is signed, keep production use within the free grant.
